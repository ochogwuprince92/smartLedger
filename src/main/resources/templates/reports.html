Financial Reports

Balance Sheet

Shows assets, liabilities, and equity at a point in time.

Income Statement

Shows revenue and expenses over a period of time.

Trial Balance

Lists all ledger account balances to verify debits equal credits.

Cash Flow Statement

Shows cash inflows and outflows from operations, investing, and financing.

Fee Collection Report

Shows fee collection status by class, term, and academic year.

Audit Trail Report

Shows all system changes and user activities for audit purposes.
